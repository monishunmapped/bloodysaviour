One platform, three ways to help

Find your way in.

Whether you want to donate, need to request, or coordinate a drive, start with the path that fits you.

♡

I'm a donor

Create a simple profile, choose when you are available, and keep a record of donations confirmed by a blood bank.

✚

I need blood

Share a request with the details a blood bank needs. We will help track it while the request is verified.

＋

I'm a partner

Blood banks can recall donors, coordinate requests and confirm donations. Colleges can organize a drive with a partner bank.

A clear, careful journey

Help, with the right people in the loop.

01

Share what’s needed

A requester shares the blood group, component, location and a way for the verification team to call back.

02

A blood bank reviews

A licensed blood bank checks the request and decides the next steps. Donor contact details stay private.

03

Donation is confirmed

Donations happen at the blood bank. Only its authorized staff can confirm a donation or issue a certificate.

Trust comes first

Some things are never up for compromise.

Built around consent, privacy and the role of licensed blood banks. This platform does not offer medical advice.

🏥 Through licensed blood banksDonors are invited to a blood bank, never directly to a patient.
🔒 Private by designFamilies never see donor phone numbers. Consent is purpose-specific.
♡ Voluntary, alwaysNo cash, vouchers or cash-value rewards for donation.
✓ Blood bank decidesOnly the blood bank decides donor eligibility and confirms donations.
